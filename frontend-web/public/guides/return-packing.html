How to pack your painting for return

Six simple steps to send it back safely.

1

Check the Shipping section

After 30 days are complete, you will receive the Shiprocket details in the Shipping section.

2

Use the original box

Place the painting back in the same box, packed the same way it arrived, to protect it from damage.

3

Add back every item

Include the hangers, nylon thread and display card that came with the painting.

4

Tape all sides securely

Tape the package properly so nothing can open or shift during shipping.

5

Paste the invoice in the same place

Print the invoice and paste it exactly where the previous delivery invoice was, to avoid confusion.

6

Hand over to the delivery partner

Give the sealed package to the delivery partner. You are all done!
